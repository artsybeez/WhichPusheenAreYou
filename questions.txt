To help you create a personalized "Which Pusheen Are You?" quiz, here is a structured layout of 12 cute and simple questions. Each question features 11 specific options that correspond directly to one of the 11 characters you requested.

At the end of the quiz, the character with the most matches will reveal who the taker is!

### **The 11 Characters & Their Personality Vibes:**

1. **Pusheen:** Lazy, loves snacks, cozy, sleeping.
2. **Stormy:** Fluffy, energetic, adventurous, wants to be just like Pusheen.
3. **Pip:** Little brother, mischievous, curious, wants to be a fierce wolf.
4. **Sunflower:** Cheerful, bright, loves nature, always smiling.
5. **Biscuit:** Soft, helpful, sweet, loves baking and treats.
6. **Sloth:** Quiet, very slow, calm, loves taking long naps.
7. **Cheek:** The hamster, loves cooking, always has food in their cheeks.
8. **Bo:** The parakeet, dreams of being a big blue bird, confident.
9. **Tommy:** The ferret, playful, sneaky, loves exploring narrow spaces.
10. **Pusheenicorn:** Magical, whimsical, loves rainbows and stardust.
11. **Pup:** Playful, loyal, loves chasing balls, full of joy.
