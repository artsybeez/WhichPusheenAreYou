To help you create a personalized "Which Pusheen Are You?" quiz, here is a structured layout of 12 cute and simple questions. Each question features 6 specific options that correspond directly to one of the 6 characters.

At the end of the quiz, the character with the most matches will reveal who the taker is!

### The 6 Characters & Their Personality Vibes:

1. **Pusheen:** Lazy, loves snacks, cozy, sleeping.
2. **Stormy:** Fluffy, energetic, adventurous, wants to be just like Pusheen.
3. **Pip:** Little brother, mischievous, curious, wants to be a fierce wolf.
4. **Cheek:** The hamster, loves cooking, always has food in their cheeks.
5. **Bo:** The parakeet, dreams of being a big blue bird, confident.
6. **Sloth:** Quiet, very slow, calm, loves taking long naps.

---

### The 12 Quiz Questions

#### 1. Pick your absolute favorite snack or treat!

* **A)** A whole box of chocolate chip cookies *(Pusheen)*
* **B)** A giant, colorful cupcake with extra frosting *(Stormy)*
* **C)** A secret stash of hidden candies *(Pip)*
* **D)** Anything I can stuff into my cheeks right now *(Cheek)*
* **E)** Tiny, perfectly round crackers *(Bo)*
* **F)** A single snack that I will take an hour to eat *(Sloth)*

#### 2. Choose your ideal weekend activity:

* **A)** Taking a 5-hour nap on a sunny spot on the rug *(Pusheen)*
* **B)** Bouncing around the room and playing dress-up *(Stormy)*
* **C)** Planning a tiny, harmless prank or building a fort *(Pip)*
* **D)** Hosting a small dinner party with lots of food *(Cheek)*
* **E)** Practicing my poses in front of a mirror *(Bo)*
* **F)** Moving very... slowly... to a new comfortable sitting spot *(Sloth)*

#### 3. What is your aesthetic or vibe?

* **A)** Comfy, cozy, and perfectly round *(Pusheen)*
* **B)** Extremely fluffy, curly, and energetic *(Stormy)*
* **C)** Small, fuzzy, and slightly chaotic *(Pip)*
* **D)** Chubby-cheeked, cute, and food-loving *(Cheek)*
* **E)** Tiny, elegant, and dreaming big *(Bo)*
* **F)** Chill, relaxed, and completely unbothered *(Sloth)*

#### 4. If you found a cardboard box, what would you do with it?

* **A)** Sit in it immediately. If I fits, I sits *(Pusheen)*
* **B)** Jump in and out of it repeatedly while making happy noises *(Stormy)*
* **C)** Hide inside it and surprise anyone who walks past *(Pip)*
* **D)** Fill it up with snacks for later *(Cheek)*
* **E)** Stand on top of it so I look tall and majestic *(Bo)*
* **F)** Crawl inside very slowly and use it as a dark nap cave *(Sloth)*

#### 5. Pick a magical superpower you'd love to have:

* **A)** The ability to summon snacks just by thinking about them *(Pusheen)*
* **B)** Infinite energy so I never have to stop playing *(Stormy)*
* **C)** Shape-shifting into a giant, fierce wolf *(Pip)*
* **D)** A pocket dimension where I can store endless food *(Cheek)*
* **E)** The ability to grow into a giant, legendary creature whenever I want *(Bo)*
* **F)** The power to stop time so my naps can last forever *(Sloth)*

#### 6. How do you react when your alarm goes off in the morning?

* **A)** Press snooze five times and pull the blanket over my head *(Pusheen)*
* **B)** Wake up instantly and do a little morning dance *(Stormy)*
* **C)** Roll out of bed and immediately start looking for adventure *(Pip)*
* **D)** My eyes open and I immediately reach for a bedside snack *(Cheek)*
* **E)** Sit up perfectly straight and prepare to conquer the day *(Bo)*
* **F)** ...What alarm? I slept right through it *(Sloth)*

#### 7. What's your favorite type of weather?

* **A)** Rain outside so I have a perfect excuse to stay in bed *(Pusheen)*
* **B)** A gentle, cool breeze that fluffs up my hair *(Stormy)*
* **C)** A cloudy, mysterious day perfect for exploring outside *(Pip)*
* **D)** A mild, pleasant day ideal for a picnic in the park *(Cheek)*
* **E)** A clear blue sky where you can see for miles *(Bo)*
* **F)** A warm, humid day perfect for lounging on a hammock *(Sloth)*

#### 8. Choose an animal you feel a special connection to (besides a cat!):

* **A)** A happy, round panda eating bamboo *(Pusheen)*
* **B)** A bouncy, fluffy bunny rabbit *(Stormy)*
* **C)** A wild, howling wolf or a tiny fox *(Pip)*
* **D)** A cute little hamster saving snacks for later *(Cheek)*
* **E)** A proud, beautiful blue bird *(Bo)*
* **F)** A slow-moving, relaxed tree sloth *(Sloth)*

#### 9. What kind of gift do you like to give your friends?

* **A)** A share of my favorite snack (but only a small bite!) *(Pusheen)*
* **B)** A handmade drawing with lots of glitter *(Stormy)*
* **C)** A cool rock or a shiny button I found *(Pip)*
* **D)** A delicious recipe book or cooking gadget *(Cheek)*
* **E)** An inspiring speech or a fancy accessory *(Bo)*
* **F)** A cozy, soft blanket for napping *(Sloth)*

#### 10. Where is your absolute favorite spot in the house?

* **A)** Directly on top of the laptop keyboard while someone is working *(Pusheen)*
* **B)** In the middle of the living room floor surrounded by toys *(Stormy)*
* **C)** Tucked away in a dark corner or a blanket fort *(Pip)*
* **D)** Near the pantry or the dining table *(Cheek)*
* **E)** Perched high up on the back of a chair to overlook the room *(Bo)*
* **F)** Nestled deep into the softest, fluffiest couch cushions *(Sloth)*

#### 11. What makes you the happiest?

* **A)** Eating a delicious meal and immediately falling asleep *(Pusheen)*
* **B)** Making people laugh and learning new things *(Stormy)*
* **C)** Growing bigger and feeling strong and independent *(Pip)*
* **D)** Trying out a brand new recipe that tastes amazing *(Cheek)*
* **E)** Achieving a big goal and showing off my hard work *(Bo)*
* **F)** Absolute peace, quiet, and slow moments *(Sloth)*

#### 12. Finally, pick a cute sound effect that describes you best:

* **A)** *Munch munch / Snoreee* *(Pusheen)*
* **B)** *Yay! / Boing!* *(Stormy)*
* **C)** *Awoo! / Rawr!* *(Pip)*
* **D)** *Nom nom / Squeak!* *(Cheek)*
* **E)** *Tweet! / Ta-da!* *(Bo)*
* **F)** *Yawnnnn / Zzz...* *(Sloth)*

---

### How to Score:

Have the person taking the quiz tally up which character letter they picked the most.

* Mostly **A** = You are **Pusheen**!
* Mostly **B** = You are **Stormy**!
* Mostly **C** = You are **Pip**!
* Mostly **D** = You are **Cheek**!
* Mostly **E** = You are **Bo**!
* Mostly **F** = You are **Sloth**!
